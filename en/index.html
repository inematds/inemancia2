Why this exists·Read before the table of contents

§A conversation notebook, not an info product

INEMA.NCIA began as a Telegram channel — scattered fragments about the neuroscience of attention, perception, habit, persuasion, and the skills that remain when AI takes over the rest.1 The problem with the chat format is that a good idea scrolls up and disappears. This course gathers those fragments, separates what’s a signal from what was just a link, and puts them back together as a text you can read at your own pace.

The channel makes the same bet: no magic formula, no promise of transformation in thirty days. There are six short, deep learning paths — from the neuroscience of focus to viral psychology — and each reads like a session, at your own pace, with the difficult details kept in the margin for when you want them.

▸ Going deeper: why “human” is the common thread optional

Optional layer — the index below is enough to get started. As the machine writes, summarizes, and generates, the differentiator shifts to what it cannot do: decide where to focus attention, read your own perception, sustain a habit without applause, persuade without manipulating. At its core, nearly the entire channel revolves around this one thesis — and it is what organizes the six tracks.
