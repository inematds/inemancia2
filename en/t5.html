Before moving on: four quick checks

No grades, no scoreboard. Answer from memory, then reveal to compare.

01Why do most persuasive messages fail?Reveal

Because they talk about the person writing — the product, the company, the competition. Real persuasion starts with the other person: their desires, fears, and beliefs.

02What are the four beats of a viral story, in order?Reveal

Hook (warning/curiosity), identification (emotional mirror), conflict (tension), and payoff (insight with a reward). Together, they close a dopamine loop.

03In FGMD, which trigger is often ignored—and why does that come at a high cost?Reveal

A understanding. If people feel stupid, they leave even when they're interested. Simplifying and translating jargon is what builds real authority.

04What makes NCIAFlux’s promise easy to explain in one sentence?Reveal

It reverses the expectation: “you don’t adapt to the method — the method adapts to your NeuroFlux.” It speaks to the user’s fears, not the features.
