Before moving on: four quick checks

No grades, no scoreboard. Answer from memory, then reveal to compare.

01Why does relying only on motivation usually fail in the medium term?Reveal

Because motivation comes from outside and is temporary — it flares up quickly and fades at the first difficulty. What sustains the result is the determination: a clear purpose, discipline, commitment, and resilience, which don't depend on being inspired.

02How can you tell a lack of permission from a simple lack of priority?Reveal

By the emotion when giving up. Relief indicates a conscious choice (priority); frustration and regret indicate an internal block—lack of permission to allow yourself to move forward.

03Why is the first hour of the day the best window for learning?Reveal

High synaptic plasticity (high dopamine, norepinephrine, and acetylcholine levels upon waking), low cognitive load (a rested prefrontal cortex), and memory consolidation reinforced by reviewing soon after sleep.

04What makes five hours a week so powerful over the long term?Reveal

The cumulative effect combined with consistency, deliberate focus, and measurement: 260 hours per year, 780 in three years, 2,600 in ten — intentional, spaced learning, not casual learning.
