Before moving on: four quick checks

No grades, no scoreboard. Answer from memory, then reveal to compare.

01Why does a beginner feel more confident than an expert?Reveal

And that's the Dunning-Kruger effect: the same lack of knowledge that leads to mistakes prevents you from notice the error. Only those who dig deeper into the topic see its complexity — and therefore become more cautious. Metacognition is what corrects the reading at both ends.

02Why can two identical products have different values?Reveal

Because perceived value depends not only on the object, but also on the context and framing. Setting, packaging, and story change how a person feels upon receiving it—and even how the product seems to work (a washed car, a brand-name painkiller).

03Which activity reinforces learning the most, according to Glasser?Reveal

Teaching others (~95%) is at the top of the pyramid. Reading is at the bottom (~10%). The more active your role — discussing, practicing, teaching — the more you retain.

04What is the point of knowing the Enneagram, MBTI, and DISC?Reveal

To identify each person’s motivation or decision-making style and adapt the message to what they value—without changing the content, only how it’s presented. It’s perception applied to communication.
