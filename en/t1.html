Before moving on: four quick checks

No grades, no scoreboard. Answer from memory, then reveal to compare.

01Why do you start noticing more red cars after wanting one?Reveal

The SRAA filters the flood of stimuli through perceived relevance. By focusing on the red car, you teach the filter to pick it out—the number of cars in the world hasn’t changed, your focus has.

02Why is social media addictive like a casino?Reveal

By the intermittent variable reward: sometimes the reward comes (a like, a notification), and sometimes it doesn't. Uncertainty keeps the user waiting for the next dose of dopamine, just like a slot machine.

03What is the biggest cost of Shiny Object Syndrome?Reveal

Switching projects before finishing wastes resources, misses opportunities, and throws away the time of ramp-up — lots of beginnings, almost no finishes. The defense is to filter ideas for alignment with the main goal.

04What changes in 2025 security, according to the learning path?Reveal

Vulnerability shifts from technical to human. Defense moves from the network perimeter to the cognitive defense — the firewall becomes the trained mind, a matter of cognitive literacy.
